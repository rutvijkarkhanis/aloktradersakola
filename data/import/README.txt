Fabrication.pdf product import package

Source: Fabrication.pdf (49 pages)

products.csv contains 37 distinct product records extracted from the PDF.
The primary_image column points to the page image used as the main product image.
gallery_pages lists continuation/detail pages belonging to the same product.

Important: Some PDF pages are continuation/detail diagrams and do not contain a separate product price. Those pages were grouped with the preceding product. The Jali Single Layer Stand on page 49 has no visible price in the PDF, so price_inr is blank.

Images are page renders from the original PDF. They preserve the product visuals and the text shown in the catalogue.
